Support

Questions, a wrong size or a tracing that won't measure? Email support@tackmatch.app and we will get back to you.

Measuring

I don't have a soft tape measure.

Wrap a piece of string at the same height, pinch it where it meets, lay it flat along a ruler and enter that length. Choose "String & ruler" in the helmet flow.

Which flexible curve should I buy?

A 60 cm (24 in) flexible curve from an art or drafting shop. 30 cm (12 in) curves are too short to reach the tree-point area.

The app says my tracing is too shallow.

Mould the curve further down both sides of the withers and trace it again. Widths are only measured as deep as the tracing reaches.

My two tracings don't agree.

Flexible curves spring back. Make sure the horse stands square on level ground, place the curve 2–3 fingers behind the shoulder blade each time, lift it off without flexing it, and trace the inside edge. A third tracing usually settles it.

How accurate is the scale?

Use Settings › Check your setup: draw a 20 cm line with a real ruler, photograph it, and TackMatch reports the length it measured.

Subscription

Restore a purchase on a new device

Open the TackMatch Pro screen or Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Cancel or change your plan

Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › TackMatch. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal or the end of a free trial to avoid a charge.

Data

Use TackMatch on iPhone and iPad

Turn on iCloud sync in TackMatch Settings on both devices. It takes effect the next time the app starts.

Send a horse's tracing to my saddle fitter

Share › Profile file creates a .tackmatch file. Your fitter opens it in TackMatch to see the tracings and results.

Report a wrong size

Every match has "Report a wrong size". It opens an email with the product and size filled in; a link to the brand's current chart helps us fix it quickly.
