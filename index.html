For horse and rider

Fit the saddle to the horse, and the boots and helmet to the rider.

TackMatch turns one wither tracing and a few tape measurements into the saddle tree width, riding helmet size and shape, and tall boot size to try first, across brands. Works on any iPhone or iPad. No LiDAR needed.

Download on the App Store

Fit the horse

Shape a flexible curve over the withers, trace it onto card beside a blank sheet of paper, and take one photo. See the wither angle, widths at set depths and the tree-width category to try first.

Fit the rider

Helmet size from head circumference, head shape with the two-book method, and tall boot height, calf and foot size, with the drop allowance leather boots need.

Share with your fitter

Send a result card, a PDF fit report or a profile file your saddle fitter can open in TackMatch. Every size shows its source and the date it was checked.

What you need: a soft tape measure, a 60 cm (24 in) flexible curve, poster board or large card, one blank Letter or A4 sheet, and two hardcover books. No tape? String and a ruler work.

Fit guide, not a guarantee. TackMatch narrows down what to try. It does not guarantee fit, certify helmet safety or assess your horse's health. Confirm with an in-person try-on and a qualified saddle fitter, and check a helmet's safety label before you ride.
