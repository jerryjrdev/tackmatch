Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

TackMatch: Saddle & Helmet Fit ("TackMatch", "we", "us") is a fit guide for horse and rider. The short version: your measurements and photos never leave your device (and your own iCloud, if you turn sync on). There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. The only data that leaves the app is your subscription purchase record, described below.

What stays on your device

  • Profiles and measurements — rider names, head, calf, leg and foot measurements, shoe size, and horse details such as name, breed, height, birth year, discipline, notes and an optional photo.
  • Wither tracings — the photo you take of a tracing, the straightened image the app makes from it, and the measured curve and results.
  • Your setup answers — the name and choices you enter during setup are stored on your device to personalise the app.

None of this is sent to us. We have no server that receives it.

iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, your profiles, measurements and tracings are stored in your private iCloud database by Apple so they appear on your other devices. That database belongs to your Apple Account; we cannot see or access it. Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud.

The camera

TackMatch uses the camera only to photograph your wither tracing and the sheet of paper beside it. The photo is processed on your device to measure the curve. It is never uploaded to us. You can also import a photo you choose; TackMatch only sees the photo you pick.

Notifications

Re-measure, re-trace and finish-later reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us to deliver them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app checks your subscription status with Apple on your device.

We use RevenueCat to keep a record of subscription purchases (for example, when a free trial starts, converts, renews or is cancelled) so we can understand how TackMatch Pro is used. RevenueCat receives your purchase history, a random anonymous ID created by the app, and basic technical information such as your device model, OS version and IP address. It does not receive your name, email, measurements, photos or profiles, and this information is not linked to your identity or used to track you across other apps. RevenueCat's own privacy policy applies to the data it processes.

Size-chart updates and links

TackMatch may download updated size charts from our website. These requests contain no personal data or identifiers; as with any web request, the server receives your IP address, which we do not log or use. When you open a brand's size chart or a "Shop this size" link, that website's own privacy policy applies. "Shop this size" links may be affiliate links; they carry no personal data from TackMatch.

Sharing you choose

Result cards, PDF reports and .tackmatch profile files are created on your device and go only where you send them (for example, to your saddle fitter by AirDrop, Messages or email).

Contacting us

If you email us (for example, to report a wrong size), we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply and to fix the size catalog.

Children and junior riders

TackMatch is not directed at children. A parent or guardian can enter a junior rider's measurements; this information stays on the device and in the parent's iCloud as described above.

Deleting your data

Delete a rider, horse or result in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. If you used iCloud sync, you can also remove TackMatch data in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If TackMatch starts collecting any other data, we will say so here and in the App Store privacy label before it does.
